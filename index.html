About Me

🧑‍💻

I'm Rahul Khanal, a passionate ethical hacker and cybersecurity researcher based in Nepal. I specialize in identifying and responsibly disclosing vulnerabilities in web applications, networks, and cloud infrastructure.

My journey into security started with curiosity — understanding how systems work under the hood — and evolved into a mission: making the digital world safer. I actively participate in bug bounty programs on platforms like HackerOne, Bugcrowd, and Intigriti.

When I'm not hunting bugs, I'm solving CTF challenges, conducting security research, or helping communities learn about cybersecurity awareness.
